I'm a backend developer in the making. I started the first year of **Systems Analysis and Development at FIAP**, and since day one my focus has been on building solid server-side applications: APIs, business rules and databases.

I work mainly with **Java and Spring Boot**, and I'm also growing my skills with **Go** and **Python**, using **PostgreSQL** for data. I enjoy turning ideas into practical solutions, learning by building real projects and writing code that is clean, efficient and easy to maintain.

### Why banking tech?

The banking sector is where backend engineering matters the most: security, reliability, performance and precision are not optional, they are the product. Every transaction has to be right, every time. That challenge is exactly what motivates me, and my goal is to become a **Software Engineer in banking technology**.
